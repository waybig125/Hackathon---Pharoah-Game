If the player find a key, he can escape, and win, by reaching a boat.
But he can also win by killing all the mummies (but it will take a hell lot of time, to kill all those mummies etc).

We can add a torch helmet, and batteries that keep the torch alive. This allows the person to use that torch to see through the fog.

When mummies die, they leave behind orbs which can be taken by the player to increase his health.
